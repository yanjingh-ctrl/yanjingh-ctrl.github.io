02 / ROBOTICS HARDWARE

Quadruped Robot

Structural design, additive manufacturing & motion validation

SolidWorksFDM / PETGDfAM

Engineering approach

Designed a scaled quadruped chassis and four articulated leg assemblies in SolidWorks. The project evaluated rapid fabrication and joint feasibility rather than powered locomotion.

Fabrication & verification

Fabricated PETG parts on Bambu Lab P1S and A1 printers; compared build orientations, assembled the chassis and manually articulated joints. SolidWorks Motion was used to inspect kinematic feasibility.

03 / UNDERWATER ROBOTICS

RoboSub AUV

Mechanical integration, buoyancy analysis & structural validation

CADFEABuoyancy

My documented contributions

Presented waterproof enclosure testing, performed a mechanical buoyancy calculation, and presented a 100 N load-case structural FEA for the team’s AUV concept during its critical design review.

Design & test status

The modular design uses aluminum extrusions, a waterproof electronics enclosure, thrusters and wheels. The CDR reports a 1 m / 10 min enclosure pressure test; navigation, speed and mission endurance had not yet been verified.
